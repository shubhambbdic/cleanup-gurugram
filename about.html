Who We Are

A community movement for a cleaner Gurugram

We organize weekly cleanup drives, restore public parks, and teach waste segregation so residents can take ownership of the city's shared spaces.

Built on collective action

Cleanup Gurugram brings neighbours, RWAs, and first-time volunteers together. Gloves, bags, and guidance are provided on-site so anyone can join, regardless of experience.

From Sector 56 markets to DLF Phase 4 lanes, we target hotspots that clog drains, spoil parks, and undermine the city we all share.

Our Core Mission

High-impact work across streets, parks, and community education.
