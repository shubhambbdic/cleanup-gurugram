The Mission

Clearing DLF Phase 4 of Single-Use Plastics

Our goal for this drive was focused and critical: clear the residential lanes and market perimeters of DLF Phase 4 from accumulated single-use plastics before the monsoon season. These plastics frequently clog local drainage systems, leading to waterlogging and localized flooding.

With the support of local residents and dedicated volunteers, we systematically targeted hotspots identified during our preliminary surveys, ensuring proper segregation and dispatch to authorized recycling facilities.
